JOB DESCRIPTION
Senior Backend Engineer

COMPANY OVERVIEW
We are a fast-growing SaaS company building the next generation of business intelligence tools. Our platform serves over 5 million users worldwide and processes billions of data points daily.

POSITION: Senior Backend Engineer
LOCATION: San Francisco, CA / Remote
TYPE: Full-time
SALARY: $150,000 - $200,000 + equity

ABOUT THE ROLE
We're looking for an experienced Senior Backend Engineer to join our Core Platform team. You'll be responsible for designing and building scalable backend services that power our analytics platform. This role requires strong technical skills, leadership experience, and a passion for building high-performance systems.

REQUIREMENTS

Must Have:
• 5+ years of professional backend development experience
• Expert-level proficiency in Node.js and TypeScript
• Strong experience with PostgreSQL and database optimization
• Hands-on experience with AWS cloud services (EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS)
• Experience building and deploying microservices
• Solid understanding of RESTful API design principles
• Experience with Docker and Kubernetes
• Strong knowledge of distributed systems and event-driven architecture
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field from an accredited university

Nice to Have:
• Experience with Python or Go
• Knowledge of GraphQL
• Experience with Redis or other caching solutions
• Familiarity with Terraform or other Infrastructure as Code tools
• Experience with monitoring tools (Prometheus, Grafana, DataDog)
• Previous experience in a leadership or mentoring role
• Open-source contributions

RESPONSIBILITIES
• Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services and APIs
• Lead architectural decisions for new features and services
• Collaborate with frontend engineers, product managers, and designers
• Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code
• Mentor junior and mid-level engineers
• Participate in code reviews and technical discussions
• Optimize application performance and database queries
• Ensure high availability and reliability of backend systems
• Contribute to technical documentation and best practices

WHAT WE OFFER
• Competitive salary and equity package
• Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
• 401(k) matching
• Unlimited PTO
• Remote-friendly work environment

• Latest MacBook Pro and equipment
• Collaborative and inclusive team culture

TECH STACK
• Backend: Node.js, TypeScript, Express.js, Python
• Databases: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis
• Cloud: AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS, SQS)
• Infrastructure: Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform
• Monitoring: Prometheus, Grafana, CloudWatch
• CI/CD: Jenkins, GitHub Actions

5. Final chat with VP of Engineering (30 minutes)
